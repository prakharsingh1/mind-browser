Onion Mode

Your trusted connection to the Tor network. This window shares nothing with your normal browsing, and is wiped when you close it.

Searches run on DuckDuckGo's onion service. No logs, no redirects.
What this does and does not protect This hides your IP address and lets you reach .onion services. It is not Tor Browser. Tor Browser makes every user look identical; this is still Mind Browser, with your window size and your extensions, so a determined site could pick you out by fingerprint even without knowing your address. Good for privacy from your network and for reaching onion services. If being identified would put you in danger, use Tor Browser instead.
